FastFood Rider

Interactive demo of the Ember design (spec §10). Demo data only; nothing is sent to a server.

  1. Sign in with any phone and an 8+ character password.
  2. Go online. A request arrives in about 5 seconds.
  3. Accept, then slide to confirm pickup.
  4. Complete delivery with code 4821, or try “Can’t deliver…”.
